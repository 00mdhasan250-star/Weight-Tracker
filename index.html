Security & Platform Guide

About NEXORA & Security Rules

100% Automated AI Cloud Mining Ecosystem

NEXORA MINING is an advanced cloud-based AI robot mining platform. Our system allows users to rent automated mining robots that generate hourly rewards in BDT/USDT/INR, complete social media tasks, spin the lucky wheel, and build a passive income network through referrals.

1. AI Robot Mining & 3-Hour Cycle

When you purchase a robot from the Marketplace, it activates immediately. Free Starter Robots can be unlocked by watching 10 rewarded ads. Each mining cycle runs for 3 hours—even if you close the app, the timer continues counting. After 3 hours, click 'Claim' and watch a short ad to credit your wallet.

2. Daily Check-In, Spins & Tasks

Check in daily to receive instant cash rewards plus 1 Free Spin. Completing 7 consecutive days unlocks the special Gift Box and 5 Bonus Spins (missing a day resets the streak). You can also visit social media links in the Tasks tab for 10 seconds to claim additional instant cash rewards.

3. Deposits & Fast Withdrawals

Always send deposits to the exact number or QR code shown on the Deposit page and submit the valid Transaction ID (TrxID). Withdrawals are processed within 1–24 hours. If a withdrawal request is rejected by the admin, 100% of the amount is automatically refunded to your wallet balance.

4. Referral Bonus & Hourly Commission

Every user receives a unique 6-character Referral Code. When a new user registers with your code, they get an instant Signup Bonus. Furthermore, whenever your referred user buys a robot and collects mining earnings, you automatically earn an hourly percentage commission!

5. Anti-Fraud & Multiple Accounts Policy

Creating multiple accounts on the same device/IP to abuse signup bonuses, using automated scripts/auto-clickers, or submitting fake deposit TrxIDs is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent account suspension.
